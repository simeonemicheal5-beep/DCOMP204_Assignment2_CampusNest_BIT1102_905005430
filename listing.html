About this room

A bright single room on the hill, five minutes on foot from campus. It has a comfortable bed, a proper desk for study, shelf space and a window with a view over the city. Water and power are reliable, and the house has a gate and a security guard at night.

The room is in a quiet house shared with other students, so it suits anyone who wants to focus on their course while staying close to everything.
